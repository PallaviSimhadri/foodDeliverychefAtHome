# BRD-FD-001 · Section 2: Objectives and success measures

> Extracted from `BRD-FD-001.pdf` (v1.0, 28 Oct 2026). The PDF is the source of truth; if this text and the PDF disagree, the PDF wins.

```text
The platform succeeds if it fulfils confirmed orders on time from accurate stock, and lets the client open new hubs
without new app releases.

2.1 Business objectives

OBJ-01 — Offer a consistent menu and predictable portions through standard ingredient kits.                                    Core

OBJ-02 — Let customers order delivery or book a chef from one app, with one account and one payment flow.                      Core

OBJ-03 — Open new hubs and cities through dashboard configuration, not code changes or app releases.                          Scale

OBJ-04 — Accept only orders the hub can fulfil, using live stock, chef and rider capacity, and charge only for what is        Stock
held.

OBJ-05 — Give partners one simple app for all their work, whether they cook, deliver or both.                              Partner

OBJ-06 — Give operations real-time visibility of orders, partners, stock, payments and exceptions.                             Ops

OBJ-07 — Keep a full record of every order, cancellation, refund and admin change for support and audit.                      Audit

   Operational Principle: Hold-Before-Payment & Configuration-Driven Scale
   Across OBJ-01 to OBJ-07, customer trust relies on reserving physical kits and verified chef slots before payment is
   captured, eliminating stock-out refunds while allowing head office to launch new hubs purely via dashboard
   configuration.
```
