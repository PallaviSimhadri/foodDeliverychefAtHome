# BRD-FD-001 · Section 3: Glossary

> Extracted from `BRD-FD-001.pdf` (v1.0, 28 Oct 2026). The PDF is the source of truth; if this text and the PDF disagree, the PDF wins.

```text
 Term                          Meaning in this document

 Hub                           A company-run kitchen and store that holds kits, chefs and riders, and fulfils orders in its service
                               zone.

 Kit                           A pre-packed, uncooked set of portioned ingredients for one dish at a set serving size.

 Dish                          A menu item the customer sees. Each dish maps to one kit (or a set of kits) and a standard
                               recipe.

 Service zone                  The area a hub serves, set as a radius, a drawn map area or a list of postal codes.

 Food Order                    The delivery service: cooked at the hub, delivered by a rider.

 Food with Chef                The home-visit service: a chef brings the kits and cooks at the customer's home.

 Order                         A confirmed Food Order.

 Booking                       A confirmed Food with Chef visit for a date and time slot.

 Slot                          A bookable time window for a chef visit, including travel and cooking time.

 Guest count                   The number of people a booking or order is for, used for minimum-order and portion rules.

 Customer app                  The mobile app customers use to order food and book chefs.

 Partner                       A chef or delivery rider approved by the client to work through the platform.

 Partner app                   The single mobile app all partners use. It shows chef screens, rider screens or both, depending
                               on the partner's approved role.

 Operations dashboard          The web app used by hub managers, support agents, finance reviewers and super admins, with
                               screens by role.

 Hold                          Kits and chef time set aside for a customer for a short time while they pay, so no one else can
                               take them.

 Visit OTP                     A one-time code the customer gives the chef on arrival, only after checking the chef and the kits
                               (see Arrival kit check). Entering it confirms the right chef is at the right home with the ordered
                               kits, and starts the visit.

 Arrival kit check             The step at the customer's door where the chef shows the sealed kits and the customer checks
                               them against the order list in the Customer app. If anything is missing, wrong or damaged, the
                               customer reports it instead of sharing the visit OTP.

 Assignment                    Linking a chef or rider to a specific order or booking.

 Exception                     Any event that stops normal fulfilment, such as a stock-out, delay, no-show or failed payment.

 Super admin                   The client's head-office user who manages all cities, hubs, menus, prices and policies.

 Priority                      Must (needed at launch), Should (strongly preferred at launch), Later (after launch).
```
