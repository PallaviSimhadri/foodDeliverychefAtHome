# BRD-FD-001 · Section 1: Executive summary

> Extracted from `BRD-FD-001.pdf` (v1.0, 28 Oct 2026). The PDF is the source of truth; if this text and the PDF disagree, the PDF wins.

```text
The platform sells freshly cooked meals two ways, both made from the same pre-packed, uncooked ingredient kits
held at company hubs:

   Food Order: the hub cooks the dishes fresh and a rider delivers them.
   Food with Chef: a chef from the hub brings the kits to the customer's home at a booked time and cooks there.

One kit model keeps the menu, portions, stock and pricing in one system, and lets each hub use its chefs for kitchen
work and home visits from one roster. There is no fixed number of hubs or cities. Each hub has its own service zone,
menu availability, kit stock, chef capacity and delivery capacity. An order goes only to a hub that can serve that
address and fulfil that service at that time, and the customer pays only once the kits and chef time are held for them.

The launch scope has four deliverables: (1) Customer app (Android and iOS) for ordering food and booking chefs; (2)
Partner app (Android and iOS), one app for chefs and delivery riders, with screens by role; (3) Operations dashboard
(web) for hub managers, support, finance and super admins, with screens by role; and (4) Shared services: orders,
bookings, scheduling, kit inventory, dispatch, payments, refunds, invoices, notifications, support and reporting. The
biggest launch decisions still open are the minimum-order rule, the chef visit fee, the cancellation policy and the
launch city (Section 20).
```
